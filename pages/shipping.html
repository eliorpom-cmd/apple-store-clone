Delivery to your door.

Next-day delivery is free on in-stock laptops, tablets, phones, earbuds and watches. Most other items ship in two days at no charge. In large cities, two-hour courier delivery is available for a small fee.

Pick up in store.

Choose a store at checkout and we email you when your order is ready. Most in-stock items are ready within an hour. You can also book a session when you collect your order.

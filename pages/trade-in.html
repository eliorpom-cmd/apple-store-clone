Quick answers about trading in.

Do I have to buy something right away?

No. You can take the credit as a gift card that you can use any time. Get an estimate with the trade-in tool and choose the gift card option.

Will my data be erased?

Yes. Once we receive your device, we erase it so it's ready for recycling or resale. Back up your data and sign out of your accounts before you send it.

What if my device is damaged?

We still check it. The estimate can change after inspection, and we tell you the new value before you decide.

How long does it take to get my credit?

Most credits are applied within a few business days after we receive and inspect your device.

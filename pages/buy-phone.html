Choose a phone in five steps.

Work through the list in order. Each step narrows the models until one stands out.

  1. Set a budget first.

    A price range removes most models at once.

  2. Pick a screen size.

    Smaller screens are easier to hold. Larger ones suit video and reading.

  3. Compare the cameras.

    Check the lenses you use most, such as wide, zoom or close-up.

  4. Check battery and storage.

    Heavy users should look at battery life. Storage should fit the apps and files you keep.

  5. Choose how to pay.

    Pay in full, pay monthly, or lease and upgrade at the end of the term.
